A quick note

BookTrack saves your list in this browser on this device using localStorage, so it remains after reloads and browser restarts. Do not clear this site's browser data or use private/incognito mode for your collection. Export backups regularly. Each device has its own list; automatic syncing between phones needs a shared online database.
